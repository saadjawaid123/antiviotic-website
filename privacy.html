Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Short version: this website collects your email only if you join the waitlist or contact us. We do not use ads, trackers or analytics, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Antiviotic is a free security app for Android and iOS that is still in development. "Antiviotic", "we" and "us" mean the team that builds it. You can reach us any time through the contact page.

What this website collects

  • Waitlist. If you join the waitlist we store your email address, the date you joined, your country (worked out from your connection, not your exact location) and your browser name. We use this only to tell you when Antiviotic launches and to stop spam sign-ups.
  • Contact form. If you send us a message we store your name, email, message and the same basic details, so we can reply.
  • Server logs. Our host, Cloudflare, briefly processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the site and protect it from attacks.

That is all. There are no analytics tools, ad pixels, social media plugins or third-party fonts on this site.

Where it is stored

Waitlist and contact entries are stored with Cloudflare, which hosts this website. Cloudflare acts as our service provider and may process data in data centres around the world. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist email until we have sent launch updates, or until you ask us to remove it. Contact messages are kept for as long as we need them to help you, and then deleted.

Who we share it with

Nobody, apart from Cloudflare as our host. We do not sell, rent or trade your data, and we never will.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or take you off the waitlist, at any time. Send a request through the contact page from the email address you used, and we will handle it within 30 days. Every launch email will also have a one-click way to unsubscribe.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies for tracking, ads or analytics, and it does not use local storage. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie to tell people apart from bots. It holds no personal profile and is not used for marketing, so no cookie banner is needed.

The Antiviotic app

The app is built so that link and file checks run on your phone. Your messages are not uploaded, read or sold, and the app has no ads. Before the app is released we will add a full section here that lists exactly what the app processes, and the same details will appear on its store listings.

Children

This website is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has joined the waitlist, contact us and we will delete the entry.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. If the change is important, we will also tell people on the waitlist by email.
